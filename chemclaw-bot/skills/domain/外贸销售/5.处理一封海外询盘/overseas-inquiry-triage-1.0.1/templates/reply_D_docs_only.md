# 模板 D · 只答单证与规格（客户明确说先别报价）

> **什么时候用**：客户信里写了 `Please send TDS/COA only, price later`、
> `We are collecting documents for our registration, no quotation needed now`，
> 或客户在走**当地注册/备案**（印尼 SNI、越南 化学品申报、印度 BIS、欧盟 REACH），
> 这一轮只需要文件。
>
> **红线**：不能因为客户没问价就顺手塞一句"我们的价格很有竞争力"——那是未经要求的商务暗示。
> 也不能承诺"我们能提供任何单证"。

---

## Subject

```
Re: {客户原主题} - Technical documents for {产品} ({用途})
```

---

## Body

```
Dear {Mr./Ms.} {姓氏},

Thank you for your inquiry dated {日期}. We note that at this stage you need
the technical documents only.

Please find below the documents and technical points we can provide for
{产品}:

- COA (Certificate of Analysis) - issued per production lot
- SDS / MSDS (GHS format)       - available
- TDS (Technical Data Sheet)    - available, {版本/年份}
{如客户提到其它单证，逐条回应：能提供 / 需另行确认 / 无法提供，三种回答，
不允许含糊过去}

Technical points as we understand your requirement:

- Product       : {产品 + 等级}
- Main content  : {纯度}
- Impurities    : {杂质}
- Particle size : {粒度}
- Packing       : {我方标准包装}

We will send the documents you listed within {LEAD_TIME_FOR_OFFER}.
If you need any document not listed above (for example a Certificate of
Origin, an ACFTA preferential certificate of origin, or third-party
inspection), please advise and we will confirm what our documentation
supports.

Best regards,

{SIGNER_NAME}
{SIGNER_TITLE} | {公司名}
```

---

## 单证回应的三种说法（不要含糊）

| 客户要的文件 | 如果能提供 | 如果不能提供 |
|---|---|---|
| COA / SDS / TDS | `We will provide ...` | 少见，通常都能给 |
| Certificate of Origin | `A Certificate of Origin can be issued by the chamber of commerce.` | — |
| ACFTA 优惠原产地证（Form E） | `We will confirm the applicable preferential certificate with our customs broker.` | 不允许写"包提供" |
| 第三方检验（SGS / BV at loading port） | `Third-party inspection at loading port can be arranged at buyer's cost - to be confirmed.` | 费用与安排必须写"待确认" |
| 生产厂审计 / 现场验厂 | `We can arrange a factory visit subject to scheduling.` | 不能承诺具体日期 |
| REACH / BIS / SNI 注册文件 | `We will check what registration documents are available for this product.` | 不能承诺"我们有注册" |

---

## 占位符

| 占位符 | 谁填 |
|---|---|
| `‹LEAD_TIME_FOR_OFFER›`（这里当"寄文件时间"用） | 销售/单证 |
| `‹SIGNER_NAME›` 等 | 销售 |

---

## 常见错误

| 错误 | 为什么错 |
|---|---|
| "Attached the TDS, our price is USD XXX" | 客户明确说不要价格，硬塞价格显得没听人说话 |
| "We can provide any certificate you need" | 单证能力承诺，报关行未必配合 |
| "Our product fully complies with SNI" | 合规结论需核实，不能凭印象说 |
| 用 "certificate of origin" 时顺手承诺 Form E | Form E 有实质性改变要求，必须核实 |
