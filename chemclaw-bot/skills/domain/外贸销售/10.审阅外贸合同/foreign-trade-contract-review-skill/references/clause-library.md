# 条款库：常见风险条款 → 说明 → 英文修改草案

每条给出：**原文长什么样 → 为什么危险 → 可以直接放进修订稿的英文草案**。

> 草案是**谈判起点**，不是终稿。具体金额、天数、机构须按项目填入并交律师确认。

---

## A. 付款类

### A1. 开证日期早于或等于签署日

**长这样**
> The Letter of Credit shall be opened not later than 15 April 2026.
> *(Date of Contract: 17 April 2026)*

**为什么危险**：付款前提客观不能履行；若交期以收证起算，整条时间链失效。

**草案**
> The Buyer shall open the Letter of Credit and have it advised to the Seller not later than 10 (ten) Business Days after the Effective Date of this Contract. "Effective Date" means the later of the dates on which both Parties have signed this Contract.

---

### A2. 逾期付款只有低息，且放弃其他救济

**长这样**
> Interest shall accrue at 5% per annum. Interest is the Seller's sole remedy and the Seller waives all rights to claim damages, suspend performance, withhold documents or avoid this Contract.

**为什么危险**：利息低于融资成本，不构成压力；中止履行、扣单、解约是出口方仅有的三个杠杆，一次放弃等于无担保赊销。

**草案**
> If the Buyer fails to pay any sum when due, interest shall accrue at SOFR plus 400 basis points per annum, calculated on the actual number of days elapsed on a 360-day basis. The Seller may also claim its actual losses, suspend performance, withhold documents including the Bill of Lading, and avoid this Contract, without prejudice to interest accrued.

---

### A3. 对方货币支付 + 对方银行定汇率

**长这样**
> If USD is not available to the Buyer, the Buyer may pay in Rupiah at the spot rate published by the paying bank, and such payment shall fully discharge the obligation.

**为什么危险**：触发条件是对方自证；汇率由对方银行单方定；差额由我方吸收。

**草案**
> All payments shall be made in United States Dollars. Payment in any other currency requires the Seller's prior written consent and shall be converted at the CNY/USD central parity rate published by CFETS on the date of payment, plus a conversion charge of 1%.

---

### A4. 放单依赖对方银行

**长这样**
> The negotiating bank shall release the original documents to the Buyer upon receipt of payment. The Seller shall not be responsible for any delay in release.

**为什么危险**：议付行是卖方的银行，不对买方负责；延误时无人可追。

**草案**
> Within 3 Business Days after receipt of payment, the Seller shall courier a full set of original documents, or a Bill of Lading marked "Surrendered" together with the carrier's release instruction. Each Party bears the storage and detention charges attributable to its own delay.

---

## B. 交付与贸易术语类

### B1. 术语无版本 / 版本被拆分

**长这样**
> CIF Jakarta. Where any other version of Incoterms is referred to in any document, that version shall govern that document.

**为什么危险**：同一批货可能同时受两个版本约束；2010 与 2020 在费用与安全义务上有实质差异。

**草案**
> The Goods are sold CIF Jakarta, Indonesia, Incoterms® 2020. All commercial documents shall state the trade term as "CIF Jakarta Incoterms® 2020". No other version of Incoterms applies to this Contract or to any document issued under it.

---

### B2. 术语与交货点自相矛盾

**长这样**
> CIF Jakarta *(clause 4.1)* … Notwithstanding, the Port of Loading is Tianjin Xingang and the Seller is not obliged to deliver the Goods to any place other than the Port of Loading. *(clause 4.3)*

**为什么危险**：CIF 下卖方负有订舱至目的港的义务；「无须在任何其他地点交货」把到岸义务抽空，两句话可被读出两个合同。

**草案**
> The Goods are sold CIF Jakarta Incoterms® 2020. The Port of Loading is [Port], China. The Seller shall contract for carriage to Jakarta and shall not be relieved of that obligation by any provision of this Contract.

---

### B3. 单方延长交期

**长这样**
> If shipment is delayed for any reason, the Buyer shall accept shipment within such extended period as the Seller may nominate.

**为什么危险**：看似保护卖方，实则常被认定为失衡条款而排除，结果反而回到一般违约赔偿（含间接损失）。

**草案**
> If shipment is delayed for reasons within the Seller's control, the Buyer may claim liquidated damages of 0.3% of the value of the delayed Goods per day, capped at 5%. If the delay is caused by an event outside the Seller's control, the delivery period is extended by the duration of the impediment, and the Seller shall notify the Buyer within 5 days.

---

### B4. 绑扎积载风险推向对方

**长这样**
> After the Goods are on board, the Buyer bears the risk of improper stowage, lashing or securing and shall indemnify the Seller.

**为什么危险**：CIF 下订约方是卖方；绑扎通常属承运人责任范围；条款三方错位。

**草案**
> The Seller, as the party contracting with the carrier, shall ensure loading, stowage and securing comply with the carrier's instructions and applicable cargo rules. Liability for stowage-related loss shall follow Article [risk] and the Bill of Lading.

---

## C. 质量与检验类

### C1. 卖方自有报告为最终依据 + 禁止对方检验

**长这样**
> The Certificate of Analysis issued by the Seller's own laboratory shall be final and binding. The Buyer shall have no right to appoint an independent surveyor.

**为什么危险**：没有任何第三方证据；一旦到港结果不符，双方都无法证明自己的主张。

**草案**
> The Seller shall issue a Certificate of Analysis, which is the basis for payment. The Buyer may, at its own cost, appoint an internationally recognised surveyor to inspect and sample the Goods at the Port of Loading before loading, attended by the Seller. A sealed retained sample of each lot shall be split between the Parties and kept for 6 months.

---

### C2. 异议期短于运输周期

**长这样**
> Any claim shall be notified within 7 (seven) days after the Bill of Lading date, failing which the Buyer irrevocably waives all claims.

**为什么危险**：货物通常仍在海上，异议期已届满；条款很可能被排除或作扩展解释，卖方反而失去确定预期。

**草案**
> Any claim on quality, specification or packing shall be notified in writing within 15 days after discharge at destination, or 30 days after the Bill of Lading date, whichever is later, supported by an independent surveyor's report. Claims for latent defects may be notified within 6 months after the Bill of Lading date.

---

### C3. 质量标准开口

**长这样**
> The Goods shall conform to Annex A and to any other requirement of the Buyer notified before shipment. The specification may be amended by the Seller after shipment without notice.

**为什么危险**：两端全开，合格与否无法判定，争议时双方各引一句。

**草案**
> The Goods shall conform to the specification in Annex A only. No other standard or requirement forms part of this Contract unless set out in a Technical Specification Annex signed by both Parties before shipment. The specification shall not be amended after shipment.

---

### C4. 规格表缺测试方法

**长这样**
> Chloride (as NaCl) | max. 0.30% | Not stated
> Bulk density | 0.90–1.10 g/cm3 | ISO 697 (method number illegible)

**为什么危险**：方法不同结果不同，卖方无法以「方法差异」抗辩。

**草案**
> For each parameter the following method applies: Na2CO3 by ISO 740; bulk density by ISO 697; loss on ignition by ISO 746; chloride by ISO 3120; iron by ISO 6353; particle size by sieving under ISO 2591-1 using ASTM E11 sieves (1.18 mm and 0.180 mm). Results shall be expressed on a dry basis.

> 方法编号示例，须按产品实际适用的标准替换。

---

### C5. 举证责任不可能完成

**长这样**
> The Buyer bears the burden of proving the non-conformity existed on board. The Buyer shall have no right to inspect at the Port of Loading.

**为什么危险**：要求证明的事，却禁止取得证据的唯一途径。

**草案**
> To enable the Buyer's proof, the Buyer may attend loading and appoint an independent surveyor at the Port of Loading, and a sealed retained sample shall be kept by both Parties for 6 months. The cost of inspection shall be borne by the Party whose position the result does not support.

---

## D. 责任与救济类

### D1. 责任上限过低

**长这样**
> The Seller's aggregate liability shall not exceed 5% of the invoice value.

**为什么危险**：单批次全损时远不足以覆盖对方损失，上限很可能被整体否定，卖方面临的敞口反而更大。

**草案**
> Each Party's liability shall be limited to the Contract Value, save for death or personal injury, wilful misconduct, gross negligence, or breach of the title and compliance warranties.

---

### D2. 责任不对等

**长这样**
> The Seller's liability is capped at 5%. The Buyer's liability is unlimited.

**为什么危险**：纸面权利无法执行（对方在境外、无担保），却给了对方要求配平其他条款的理由。

**草案**
> Each Party's liability shall be limited to the Contract Value on the same basis. The Buyer shall pay an advance payment of 10% of the Contract Value within 10 Business Days after the Effective Date, set off against the first invoice.

---

### D3. 排除一切救济

**长这样**
> The Buyer's sole remedy is price reduction or replacement, at the Seller's option. The Buyer shall not be entitled to reject, return, refuse payment or claim consequential loss.

**为什么危险**：与 D1、单方解约权叠加后形成救济真空，可能被整体推翻。

**草案**
> If the Goods do not conform, the Buyer may elect: (a) replacement at the Seller's cost; (b) a price reduction proportionate to the shortfall; or (c) if the non-conformity is fundamental, rejection of the affected lot with a refund. Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

---

### D4. 单方解约权

**长这样**
> The Buyer may terminate only where a final arbitral award has so determined. The Seller may terminate immediately on any breach.

**为什么危险**：极端不对称，对方必然要求配平。

**草案**
> Either Party may terminate by written notice if the other commits a material breach not remedied within 30 days after written notice. The Seller may terminate immediately if the Buyer fails to open the Letter of Credit within 15 days after the due date or fails to pay any sum when due.

---

### D5. 单方重新议价条款

**长这样**
> If the Seller's cost increases by more than 15%, the Seller may require renegotiation; failing agreement within 10 days, the Seller may terminate.

**为什么危险**：名为「艰难情势」，实为单方向的价格选择权（涨了走条款、跌了不回调）。

**草案**
> If a change in law, tariff or freight rate increases either Party's documented cost of performance by more than 10%, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith for 30 days. Absent agreement, either Party may terminate the unperformed portion without liability. The mechanism shall operate in both directions.

---

## E. 合规与单证类

### E1. 目的国合规全推给对方

**长这样**
> The Seller shall not be liable for any permit, licence, standard, labelling or certification requirement of the importing country. The Buyer warrants it holds all such approvals.

**为什么危险**：免责不能消灭风险；货物被扣时损失先落在持货与持产品信息的一方。

**草案**
> Each Party is responsible for approvals in its own jurisdiction. Within 10 Business Days after signing, the Seller shall provide a compliance pack (SDS, COA, composition, transport classification statement, packaging declaration) and shall reasonably cooperate with the Buyer's registration or notification applications.

---

### E2. 拒绝提供优惠原产地证

**长这样**
> The Seller shall not be obliged to provide a Form E or any preferential certificate.

**为什么危险**：对方丧失协定税率，差额会变成降价要求或谈判筹码。

**草案**
> The Seller shall apply for and provide a Certificate of Origin (Form E) under ACFTA, or an equivalent RCEP certificate where that route offers a lower rate, at its own administrative cost.

---

### E3. SDS 语言与准确性

**长这样**
> The SDS and labels shall be in English only. The Buyer shall translate and bear all consequences of an inaccurate translation. The Seller is not responsible for the accuracy of the SDS.

**为什么危险**：SDS 是运输、清关与事故追责的核心文件，「对准确性不负责」几乎不可能成立。

**草案**
> The Seller shall provide a 16-section GHS-compliant Safety Data Sheet in English and, on written request and at the Buyer's cost, in the destination language. The Seller warrants the SDS accurately describes the hazard classification as at the date of shipment.

---

## F. 争议解决类

### F1. 仲裁完全单边

**长这样**
> Arbitration: CIETAC, seat Beijing, language Chinese, sole arbitrator appointed by the Secretary-General.

**为什么危险**：所有参数单向；对方抗辩时裁决可能被拒承认，赢了执行不了。

**草案**
> Disputes shall be finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC) under the SIAC Rules in force at filing. Seat: Singapore. Language: English. Tribunal: three arbitrators. The award is final and binding.

> 选择 SIAC 只是中立选项之一；也可选 HKIAC、ICC。关键是**双方都能接受且裁决在对方国家可执行**。

---

### F2. 协商前置起算点不明

**长这样**
> The Parties shall negotiate for 60 days from the date the dispute arises. Neither Party may commence arbitration during that period.

**为什么危险**：「争议发生日」无客观标准；可能被用作拖延与管辖权异议。

**草案**
> Either Party may give a written Notice of Dispute specifying the dispute and relief sought. The Parties shall negotiate in good faith for 30 days from the date of the Notice. On expiry, either Party may commence arbitration. Nothing prevents either Party from seeking interim measures at any time.

---

### F3. 仲裁与法院并存

**长这样**
> Disputes go to arbitration *(15.3)*. The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of China for all matters not subject to arbitration *(15.5)*.

**为什么危险**：边界不清，可能出现两地并行程序。

**草案**
> All disputes shall be finally resolved by arbitration under Article [x], to the exclusion of any court. Each Party waives any right to bring proceedings in any court except to enforce an award or obtain interim measures.

---

### F4. 协商期间付款义务不明

**长这样**
> *(合同未约定争议期间是否仍须付款)*

**为什么危险**：对方可以「有争议」为由拖款，而我方已放弃中止与扣单权。

**草案**
> During any dispute the Buyer shall continue to perform all payment obligations when due and interest shall continue to accrue. Payment of amounts not in dispute shall not be withheld.

---

## G. 语言与效力类

### G1. 只做英文版

**长这样**
> This Contract is executed in English only. No Indonesian-language version is required.

**为什么危险**：部分国家对涉及本国主体的合同有本地语言要求，后果（可撤销/不可执行/仅参考）各国差异极大，须当地律师确认。

**草案**
> This Contract is executed in English and [local language], both signed and equally authentic. In the event of discrepancy, the English version prevails.

> 具体采用「双语同等有效 + 以英文为准」还是「仅以英文为准」，
> 取决于当地法是否允许约定优先语言——**必须由当地律师确认后再删改**。

---

### G2. 邮件可否构成修改

**长这样**
> No amendment is effective unless signed by both Parties. *(next sentence)* An amendment may be agreed by e-mail, and an e-mail from an officer shall be deemed signed.

**为什么危险**：自相矛盾，等于给业务员留了一个可被主张的口子。

**草案**
> No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by authorised representatives of both Parties. E-mail correspondence shall not constitute an amendment, but may evidence an amendment agreed by both Parties in writing.

---

## H. 危险化学品（适用时）

### H1. 「双方确认非危险货物」但无依据

**长这样**
> The Parties acknowledge that the Goods are not dangerous goods. No IMDG documentation is required. The Seller is not responsible for the accuracy of the SDS.

**为什么危险**：申报不符会被船公司索赔、港口处罚甚至拒载。

**草案**
> The Parties acknowledge the Goods are not classified as dangerous goods under the IMDG Code, as confirmed by Section 14 of the SDS dated [date], annexed as Annex D. Each Party shall declare consistently with that classification and is responsible for loss arising from its own inaccurate declaration.

---

### H2. 危申报责任未分配

**长这样**
> *(合同未约定谁负责危险货物申报及申报错误的责任)*

**草案**
> The Seller shall be responsible for the accuracy of the dangerous goods declaration and for providing the DG packing certificate. The Buyer shall provide timely accurate consignee details. Each Party bears loss caused by its own inaccurate information.
