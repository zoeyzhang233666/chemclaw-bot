# 05 供应商更正函模板（英文）

## 一、写信原则

1. **只写已确证的不一致/缺失/计算错误**，不可比项（PI 与 CI 金额、SDS 标准装运数据等）一句都不提——提了对方会反过来质疑你专业度；
2. **每条都要能指回文件与页码**，让对方无法推诿；
3. **给明确的动作与期限**（一般 3 个工作日），并说明不更正的后果（付款暂缓/报关延误/滞港费由谁承担——按合同条款写，不夸大）；
4. **不指控欺诈**，措辞是 "please correct / please confirm"；
5. 结尾留痕：引用我方案号，要求"回复本邮件并附改后文件"。

## 二、通用模板

```text
Subject: Request for Correction of Import Documents — Case {CASE_NO}
Our Ref.: {CASE_NO}    Date: {DATE}

Dear Sir or Madam,

Reference is made to our purchase of {PRODUCT} (CAS {CAS}), HS Code {HS},
quantity {QTY} MT at USD {UNIT_PRICE}/MT {INCOTERM}, shipped per B/L {BL_NO},
vessel {VESSEL} {VOYAGE}, ETD {ETD}, ETA {ETA}.

Upon cross-checking the full set of shipping documents, we have identified the following
points that require your correction or confirmation before we can proceed with payment,
customs declaration and vessel arrival arrangements. Please treat this as urgent.

1. [DISCREPANCY] {ITEM_1}
   Documents affected: {DOC_LIST_1}
     - {DOC_A}, page {P}: "{VALUE_A}"
     - {DOC_B}, page {P}: "{VALUE_B}"
   Action requested: {ACTION_1}

2. [DISCREPANCY] {ITEM_2}
   ...

3. [MISSING] Document not received: {DOC_NAME}
   Basis of requirement: Clause {X} of the Sales Contract / L/C clause {Y}.
   Action requested: Please send the original {DOC_NAME} by courier and a scanned copy
   by e-mail so that we can complete the customs declaration.

4. [ARITHMETIC] {DOC}: Amount does not equal Unit Price x Quantity
   {QTY} MT x USD {UNIT_PRICE}/MT = USD {EXPECTED}, whereas the document shows
   USD {ACTUAL} (difference USD {GAP}).
   Action requested: Please re-issue the Commercial Invoice so that Amount equals
   Unit Price x Quantity, and confirm the correct total payable.

5. [TO BE CONFIRMED] {ITEM}
   Action requested: Please confirm in writing which route applies, so that we may
   align our payment instruction and advise our bank accordingly.

Please note that no items that are legitimately different by the nature of the documents
(e.g. Proforma Invoice versus Commercial Invoice amounts, standard-shipment data in
Section 14 of the SDS) have been raised in this letter.

Please return the corrected documents within three (3) working days, quoting our
reference number above.

Yours faithfully,
______________________
{BUYER_NAME}
USCC: {USCC}
```

## 三、分场景话术（可直接抄）

### ① 让卖方重开发票（金额错）
> The Commercial Invoice {NO} shows a total of USD {ACTUAL}, while {QTY} MT x USD {PRICE}/MT
> equals USD {EXPECTED}. The amount in words matches the figure in the total box, which
> indicates a pricing/entry error rather than a typing slip. Please re-issue the invoice;
> we are unable to release payment against the current version.

### ② 让船代/船公司改提单（批号、收货人、重量）
> The B/L {NO} states batch {B/L_BATCH} under "Description of Goods", whereas the
> Packing List and Certificate of Analysis both certify batch {REAL_BATCH}, which is
> the batch actually loaded per the tank loading records. Please issue an amendment to
> the B/L, or issue a Letter of Indemnity acceptable to the carrier.

### ③ 催原产地证
> We have not yet received the Certificate of Origin. Please note that under Clause 5.2 of
> the Sales Contract it is a document to be presented with the shipping documents, and it
> is required for the origin element of our import declaration. Please courier the original
> and send a scan today.

### ④ 规格/COA 不符
> The Certificate of Analysis {COA_NO} states a specification of {COA_SPEC} while the
> Contract requires {CONTRACT_SPEC}. Please either provide the analysis certificate for the
> contract grade, or confirm in writing that the specification has been amended, in which
> case we will need an amended contract for our internal approval.

### ⑤ 付款方式待确认（发给买方/业务，不发给供应商）
> The Contract provides for T/T 30% in advance and 70% against copy of B/L, whereas the
> Commercial Invoice refers to L/C No. {LC} at sight. Please confirm in writing whether the
> L/C route is to be applied to this shipment (and the contract accordingly amended), or
> whether the T/T arrangement remains in force. Please also confirm whether any advance
> payment has been made.

## 四、不能说的话（红线圈）

| ❌ 不要写 | ✅ 改写为 |
|---|---|
| "This is a fraudulent document." | "The figures on the two documents do not reconcile; please clarify." |
| "Customs will definitely reject this shipment." | "This discrepancy affects the origin/price element of our import declaration, and we may be required to supplement documentation." |
| "You must bear all demurrage." | "Any costs arising from the delay in providing corrected documents will be discussed in accordance with Clause {X}." |
| "Proforma Invoice amount differs from Invoice — please correct." | （不要提，这是不可比项） |
